POST 2 — USE CASES SUMMARY
===========================================

Everyone's used to AI writing emails. Fewer people have seen what it looks like scoring a business decision in real time.

Here's where a classifier like JEV actually earns its place in a construction or property business — not as a chatbot, but as the thing quietly checking your numbers against reality:

💰 Cost & financial control
→ Matching invoices against quotes, flagging cost overruns, catching duplicate billing, scoring cash flow risk

🏗️ Site progress & scheduling
→ Reading site photos against milestones, flagging schedule slippage, scoring weather delay exposure, confirming a trade is actually ready to start

📦 Procurement & supply chain
→ Triaging supplier quotes, predicting late deliveries, catching material substitutions before they become a problem

⚠️ Risk, compliance & safety
→ Checking certificates and insurances are current, triaging incident severity, scoring contract clauses for risk before you sign

📧 Communications & admin
→ Sorting inboxes by invoice / RFI / complaint / urgent, flagging which RFIs are actually blocking work

📈 Sales & pipeline
→ Qualifying leads and tenders by fit and win probability, flagging thin-margin quotes before they go out

Same underlying mechanism every time: feed it structured and unstructured data, score it against what's already known, get a decision back in 0.1 seconds instead of a day of admin.

That's the difference between running a business on gut feel and running it on real-time certainty.
