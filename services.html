01 / Revenue

CRM & revenue systems

Design the commercial system around how clients are actually acquired, qualified, sold and retained. Particularly effective for referral-led businesses that have grown faster than their sales infrastructure.

  • CRM architecture and pipeline stages
  • Lead capture, referral attribution and follow-up
  • Sales process automation and reporting
  • SEO and search visibility systems
  • Client lifecycle and retention workflows
  • Management dashboards for commercial activity
